## Diagrams
When you finish or significantly update an Excalidraw diagram:
1. Export it with: excalidraw-canvas export --out docs/diagrams/<descriptive-name>.excalidraw
2. Save a PNG next to it with: excalidraw-canvas screenshot --out docs/diagrams/<descriptive-name>.png
3. Tell me both paths.
Use lowercase names with hyphens, e.g. payment-service-architecture.


## Writing Style
- Always write all documents and chat replies in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English.
- Use short sentences, active voice, and one idea per sentence.
- Do not use idioms, jargon, or unnecessary words.
